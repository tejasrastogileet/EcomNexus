## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report presents an analysis of e-commerce performance, highlighting key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational risks.

The analysis reports total revenue of **15,843,553.24**, generated from **96,461 orders** and **99,441 customers**. While the business demonstrates substantial revenue activity, opportunities remain to improve customer retention and operational efficiency.

The reported late delivery rate is **8.11%**, indicating a potential area for operational improvement. Revenue is concentrated in several leading product categories, while the reported repeat customer rate of **2.82%** suggests an opportunity to strengthen customer loyalty.

This report outlines five strategic recommendations to improve revenue performance, customer engagement, and operational efficiency, along with key risks requiring attention.

## CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS

Customer engagement metrics indicate an opportunity to improve repeat purchasing behavior.

The report identifies **99,441 total customers**, of whom **2,800** are classified as repeat customers. This results in a reported repeat customer rate of **2.82%**.

This suggests that customer acquisition has occurred, but repeat purchasing may be limited within the observed dataset. Potential contributing factors include customer satisfaction, product relevance, delivery experience, and post-purchase engagement.

The reported revenue per customer is **159.33**. Improving repeat purchasing behavior could increase customer lifetime value and reduce dependence on acquiring new customers.

Further analysis of repeat customer purchasing patterns may help identify opportunities for cross-selling, upselling, and personalized engagement.
